## Ideal Dimensions for a Computer Desk

Choosing dimensions is the fundamental step in selecting your computer furniture. The National Institute for Research and Safety (INRS) recommends precise minimum dimensions to guarantee optimal comfort.

### Work Surface Height

The standard height of 72 cm is designed for a person measuring 175 cm. This standard poses a major problem: it does not suit the majority of users.

**Recommendations according to height:**

- **1.63 m to 1.67 m**: opt for a 64 cm high desk
- **1.68 m to 1.74 m**: choose a 66 cm high furniture piece
- **Over 1.75 m**: a height of 71 cm will suit

The gap between your seat and the desktop should be between 20 and 26 cm to allow a natural forearm posture.

### Optimal Depth and Width

Professional computer desks must offer enough space to accommodate screen, keyboard, mouse and documents. INRS recommends:

- **Minimum width**: 120 cm for a standard workstation
- **Recommended width**: 160 to 200 cm for dual monitors or executive desk
- **Depth**: 80 cm minimum to position the screen at correct distance

A depth of 80 cm allows placing the screen about 60 cm from the eyes, the recommended distance to prevent eye fatigue.

### Leg Space

An often overlooked but essential point: space under the desk. Allow at least 75.5 cm of clearance for legs. This space allows for regular position changes, an essential condition for avoiding muscle stiffness.

## Different Types of Computer Desks

The market offers several computer desk configurations adapted to different uses and space constraints.

### The Classic Straight Desk

The most common configuration, the straight desk offers a linear work surface. It is perfectly suited to standard spaces and integrates easily into any type of layout.

**Advantages:**
- Simple and flexible installation
- Suitable for small spaces
- Generally accessible price

**Limitations:**
- Limited work surface
- Less suitable for multi-screen configurations

### The Corner Desk

The corner computer workstation intelligently exploits often underused room corners. This configuration offers an extended work surface while optimizing floor space.

**Advantages:**
- Large work surface
- Facilitated organization with distinct zones
- Ideal for multitasking

The UFIPA 2025 Barometer confirms this trend: 71% of workers would like to work more dynamically, regularly standing or walking.

**Advantages:**

## How to Set Up Your Computer Workstation

Beyond choosing the furniture itself, the overall layout of your workspace determines your daily comfort.

### Screen Positioning

The screen support should be positioned perpendicular to windows to avoid reflections. The top of the screen should be at eye level, slightly tilted back (10 to 20 degrees).

This position helps maintain an upright head and reduces neck tension. A study by Cornell University's Design Research Center demonstrates that correct positioning reduces eye fatigue by 51%.

### Work Surface Organization

A well-organized computer desk facilitates work and reduces unnecessary movements:

- **Main zone**: keyboard and mouse, directly in front of you
- **Secondary zone**: frequently consulted documents, within reach
- **Peripheral zone**: occasionally used items

Essential accessories such as monitor stand, wrist rest or document holder help optimize your posture.

## Materials and Build Quality

The choice of materials influences the durability, aesthetics and comfort of use of your computer furniture.

### The Desktop

Professional computer desks offer different types of desktops:

- **Melamine**: economical and easy to maintain, ideal for standard uses
- **High-pressure laminate**: more resistant to scratches and heat
- **Solid wood**: noble and durable, for prestige offices
- **Tempered glass**: modern and design, requires regular maintenance

Desktop thickness (generally 18 to 25 mm) determines its strength and ability to support heavy equipment.

### The Frame

The desk structure must guarantee stability and durability:

- **Steel**: robust and stable, ideal for intensive use
- **Aluminum**: lightweight and corrosion resistant
- **Wood**: warm aesthetics, suitable for classic environments

For intensive professional use, prefer metal frames with epoxy coating resistant to impacts.

### Finishes

Details make the difference: rounded edges to prevent injuries, integrated cable management for an orderly desk, leveling feet to compensate for floor irregularities.

## Practical Tips for Buying

Before purchasing your computer desk, follow these expert recommendations.

### Assess Your Real Needs

Ask yourself the right questions:

1. **What equipment do you need to accommodate?** One screen, two screens, a desktop computer?
2. **What is your space configuration?** Measure the available location precisely
3. **How many hours per day do you work at this station?** The more intensive the use, the more quality matters
4. **What are your physical constraints?** Height, existing back problems

### Test Before Buying

When possible, try the desk with your usual chair. Check that the height allows a natural posture and that the leg space is sufficient.

## Best Practices for Daily Use

Even with the best computer desk, certain habits remain essential.

### Regular Breaks

INRS recommends 5-minute breaks every hour for intensive tasks, or 15 minutes every 2 hours for less demanding tasks. Use these breaks to stand up, stretch and look into the distance.

### Alternating Postures

If you have an adjustable desk, alternate between sitting and standing every 30 to 60 minutes. This dynamic stimulates blood circulation and prevents stiffness.

### Space Organization

Keep your computer workstation tidy. A Stanford University study reveals that employees working in well-organized spaces improve their concentration by 23%.
