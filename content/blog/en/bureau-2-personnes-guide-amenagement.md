A **shared workstation** addresses concrete needs of modern businesses. Space optimization, easier colleague interaction, and reduced setup costs rank among the primary motivations.

A thoughtfully planned **double desk** delivers several measurable benefits:

- **Increased flexibility**: Simple reconfiguration as teams evolve

## Dimensions and Standards for a 2 Person Desk

Ergonomic workplace design follows established guidelines that ensure worker comfort and safety. These standards have evolved to incorporate new practices related to sit-stand desks and ergonomic furniture.

### Recommended Minimum Space

For a **dual workstation**, recommendations vary by configuration:

| Configuration | Space per Person | Minimum Total Space |
|--------------|------------------|---------------------|
| Standard shared office | 120 sq ft (11 m²) | 240 sq ft (22 m²) |
| Double desk with storage | 130-160 sq ft (12-15 m²) | 260-320 sq ft (24-30 m²) |
| Noisy shared environment | 160 sq ft (15 m²) | 320 sq ft (30 m²) |

These areas include circulation zones and storage space necessary for proper workspace function.

### Desktop Dimensions

Each position within a **2 person desk** should meet minimum dimensions:

- **Minimum width**: 48 inches (120 cm) per person (ideally 55-63 inches / 140-160 cm)
- **Depth**: 24 to 32 inches (60-80 cm) to accommodate monitor, keyboard, and documents
- **Standard height**: 28-30 inches (72-75 cm), or adjustable from 26-49 inches (65-125 cm) for sit-stand models

A bench desk measuring 8 to 12 feet (2.4-3.6 m) in total length comfortably accommodates two colleagues side by side with adequate space for their equipment.

### Circulation Space

Standards specify precise passage widths:

- **32 inches (80 cm) minimum** for single-person passage
- **60 inches (150 cm)** to allow two people to pass each other
- **40 inches (100 cm) clearance** behind each chair for easy movement

These dimensions ensure smooth circulation and prevent the claustrophobic feelings that harm wellbeing.

## Configuring an Ergonomic Double Desk

Ergonomics in a **shared workstation** extends beyond dimensions. Several factors contribute to user comfort and prevention of [musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs)](https://kwesk.com/en/blog/ergonomie/chaise-ergonomique-prevention-tms-maux-de-dos), which account for 55% of workplace injury-related emergency room visits.

### Monitor Positioning

Each user's screen should be:

- Placed directly in front (avoiding neck twisting)
- At a distance of 20 to 28 inches (50-70 cm) from the eyes
- With the top of the screen at eye level or slightly below
- Perpendicular to windows to minimize glare

In a **double desk** arrangement, ensure one colleague's screen doesn't create visual distraction for the other.

### Acoustic Management

Noise constitutes one of the primary irritants in shared spaces. For a peaceful **2 person desk**:

- Install acoustic divider panels between workstations
- Choose absorbent coverings (carpet, acoustic tiles)
- Provide noise-canceling headphones or nearby quiet zones
- Select quiet equipment (keyboards, printers)

## Furniture Suited for Shared Desks

Furniture choice determines the success of your setup. Trends for 2025-2026 favor modularity and sustainability.

### Bench Desks: The Preferred Solution

The bench desk represents the most common configuration for a **double desk**. This furniture offers:

- **Continuous desktop** allowing side-by-side installation
- **Shared base** reducing footprint
- **Partitioning options** with removable divider screens
- **Integrated cable management** for an organized space

[Sit-stand desks](https://kwesk.com/en/blog/ergonomie/bureau-assis-debout) have become standard in 2026, allowing users to alternate between sitting and standing throughout the day. This is particularly valuable in shared configurations where users have different postural preferences.

### Face-to-Face Configuration

The alternative to the bench desk is face-to-face configuration, with two individual workstations opposite each other:

**Advantages**:

- Direct communication made easier
- Sense of equity (identical workstations)
- Option to reconfigure into individual stations

**Disadvantages**:

- Requires central divider for visual privacy
- Increased risk of mutual distraction
- Slightly larger space requirement

### Shared and Individual Storage

A functional **2 person desk** incorporates suitable storage solutions:

- **Individual mobile pedestals** under the desk for personal items
- **Shared cabinets** accessible from both positions
- **Wall shelving** optimizing vertical space
- **Personal lockers** for flex office arrangements

## Lighting and Ambiance for Your Double Workstation

Lighting directly influences concentration and visual comfort. A **dual workstation** demands particular attention to this parameter.

### Natural Light

Position the **shared desk** to benefit from natural light without glare:

- Ideal orientation perpendicular to windows
- Blinds or solar films to modulate intensity
- Avoid positioning with back to window (backlight on screen)

Biophilic design, integrating natural light and vegetation, has emerged as a strong trend for workplace wellbeing.

### Artificial Lighting

Complement natural light with:

- **General lighting** of 300-500 lux for ambient illumination
- **Individual desk lamps** allowing personal adjustment
- **Neutral color temperature** (4000K) for screen work
- **Intensity variation** throughout the day

Each occupant of a **double desk** should be able to adjust their task lighting without impacting their neighbor.

## Mistakes to Avoid When Setting Up

Certain pitfalls regularly compromise **shared desk** projects.

### Underestimating Space Needs

An overly cramped **dual workstation** generates:

- Tension between colleagues
- Concentration difficulties
- Sense of crowding harmful to productivity

Respect minimum standards, or exceed them if budget allows.

### Neglecting Acoustics

Ambient noise significantly reduces efficiency. Investing in acoustic solutions during initial design prevents costly later corrections.

### Forgetting Adaptability

Teams change, needs evolve. Choose modular furniture allowing reconfiguration of the **double desk** without complete replacement.

### Selecting Unsuitable Furniture

## FAQ About 2 Person Desks

### How much space should I plan for a 2 person desk?

Ergonomic standards recommend approximately 120 square feet (11 m²) per person in a shared office, meaning 240 square feet (22 m²) minimum for two workstations. For optimal comfort with storage, plan for 260 to 320 square feet (24-30 m²).

### What desk width for two people side by side?

A bench desk of 8 to 12 feet (2.4-3.6 m) in length allows two colleagues to work with individual workspace of 4 to 6 feet (120-180 cm) each.

### How can I reduce noise in a shared desk?

Install acoustic panels between workstations, choose absorbent coverings, and equip colleagues with noise-canceling headphones for intensive concentration phases.

### Face to face or side by side: which to choose?

Side-by-side configuration limits visual distractions and offers more privacy. Face-to-face facilitates direct exchanges but requires a central divider. Your choice depends on task nature and required collaboration level.
