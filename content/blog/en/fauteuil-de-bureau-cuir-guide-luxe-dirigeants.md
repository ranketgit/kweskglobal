In 2026, the market offers three main leather categories, each with distinct characteristics:

**1. Full-Grain Leather (Top Grain Leather)**
- Superior quality with visible natural grain
- Exceptional durability (15+ years)
- Price: $1,650 - $2,750

**2. Corrected Grain Leather**

## Maintenance and Longevity: Preserving Your Investment

### Daily Leather Maintenance

To guarantee the durability of your **leather office chair**:

**Weekly routine:**
- Dusting with dry microfiber cloth
- Immediate stain cleaning with leather-specific product
- Avoid direct sun exposure (discoloration)
- Maintain stable room temperature (64-72°F)

**Monthly maintenance:**
- Application of leather conditioning cream
- Mechanism verification and screw tightening
- Lubrication of pivot points if necessary

**Semi-annual treatment:**
- Deep cleaning with professional products
- Complete inspection of ergonomic components
- Verification of alignment and adjustments

### Repair vs Replacement: When to Decide?

With a 5-year warranty and 10-15 year durability, a professional **leather office chair** remains repairable in most cases:
